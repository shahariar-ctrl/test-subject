// about us

Built by students, for students

Gradzz started as a late-night side project because doing National University's CGPA math by hand — across four years, ten grade bands and two different syllabuses — was genuinely not it. So we built a CGPA calculator that actually gets it right, and actually looks like something made in 2026.

Every department across BSc, BBA, BSS and BA Honours is covered, for both the 2024–2025 and 2013–2014 syllabuses, plus the Degree Pass course. Everything is grade-weighted and calculated live as you type — no sign-up, no spreadsheet, no cap.

Gradzz is an independent educational project and isn't affiliated with National University Bangladesh — just students trying to make results day a little less stressful for everyone else.

// privacy policy

Your grades stay yours

Gradzz doesn't ask for an account, an email, or a password — so there's nothing to leak in the first place. The grades you enter are saved only in your own browser's session storage, on your own device, for as long as that browser tab stays open.

Nothing you type into the calculator is sent to a server, sold, or shared with anyone, including us. Closing the tab, or hitting the "Reset" button on a marksheet, clears that saved data for good.

// what we don't collect

No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no passwords. We don't run ad trackers, don't build user profiles, and don't sell or share data with advertisers — there simply isn't any to sell.

If you email us or message us through the Contact page, we only use that information to reply to you — never for marketing, and never passed on to third parties.

// how session storage works

Your grades are stored using your browser's built-in session storage, tied to this specific tab. They're not synced to any account, so they won't automatically follow you to another phone, laptop, browser, or even a new tab.

Closing the tab, using private/incognito mode, or hitting the "Reset" button on a marksheet will permanently erase that saved data — we have no copy to restore it from. Download your result as a PDF before you close the tab if you want to keep it.

// cookies & analytics

Gradzz doesn't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically generate basic technical logs (such as page requests and general traffic volume) for security and uptime purposes — these logs don't contain your grades or any personal identifiers.

If that ever changes — say, adding privacy-friendly, non-identifying analytics to see which features people actually use — this page will be updated to reflect it.

// third-party links

Occasional links (like the mailto link on Contact) point outside Gradzz. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites or services you reach through those links — check their own policies if you're unsure.

// who this is for

Gradzz is built for current and prospective National University Bangladesh students, generally university-aged. It isn't designed or marketed for young children, and we don't knowingly collect any data from them — largely because we don't collect personal data from anyone in the first place.
